## S5 — Daily Experience (THE DEMO)

Point to progressive profiling prompt at top of phone:
> "See this — before today's card, one quick question. 'Do you run hot or cold?' That's the progressive profiling we talked about. Three options, tap one, done. By week two you have a full profile."

Point to food card:
> "Then one food suggestion, two sentences. 15 seconds."

Click **"Why?"**:
> "Tap Why? for the three-tradition breakdown. When they all agree, that's a trust signal."

Switch to **"Disagreement"** tab, click **"Why?"**:
> "When they disagree — like acid reflux — we show it. The app follows the safer recommendation but tells you about the disagreement. This is what makes people come back."

## S6 — Contradictions

> "The contradiction engine is the real product. Food is the daily hook. The Why panel — especially disagreements — is the value. No other app surfaces this."

## S7 — Architecture (quick)

> "The AI never picks the food. Structured database handles that. AI only explains. No hallucination risk on food selection."

## Quick Answers

**"What about mobile?"**
> Web first as test phase. If it validates, mobile is the real target. The design is already mobile-first.

**"What do Dev and Rishi work on?"**
> Phase 1 is assessment UI + scoring algorithms. Divisible across the team.

**"What about the ethnicity/food thing?"**
> The food database isn't locked to any cuisine. It types properties (warming, grounding) and matches those to foods from any cuisine. "What food background do you come from?" is a natural progressive profiling question.
